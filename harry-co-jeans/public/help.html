The First 6 Months

Wear daily without washing to allow natural high-contrast honeycombs and whiskering to form.

Cold Water Soak

Turn inside out, submerge in cold water with mild castile detergent for 30 minutes. Never machine spin violently.

Hang dry upside down in shaded breeze. Never use high heat tumble dryers on raw selvedge.
