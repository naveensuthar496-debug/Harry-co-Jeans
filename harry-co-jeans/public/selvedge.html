THE SELVEDGE SERIES

An exploration of Japanese shuttle loom weaving, natural rope dyeing, and bespoke union special construction.

CHAPTER I · THE LOOM

VINTAGE TOYODA SHUTTLE WEAVING

Modern projectile looms weave fabric at blinding speeds, producing uniform but sterile sheets. Our denim is woven on restored 1950s Toyoda G-9 shuttle looms in Okayama at a deliberate, rhythmic pace.

The low warp tension creates "loom chatter"—subtle slubs and irregularities that give every square inch of our denim authentic depth and tactile character.

CHAPTER II · THE DYE

16-DIP NATURAL ROPE DYEING

The cotton yarns are bundled into thick ropes and dipped sixteen times into deep indigo vats, oxidized in air between each dip. The pure white core of the yarn remains untouched by dye.

As you wear your pair through months and years, the dark surface gently rubs away, revealing the pure cotton beneath in sharp whiskering and honeycombs.
