International Shipping Rates & Times

Last updated: Oct 24, 2023 by Admin

We ship worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on the destination country and the selected shipping method.

Standard Shipping

  • Europe: 5-7 business days (€10 flat rate)
  • North America: 7-10 business days ($15 flat rate)
  • Rest of World: 10-14 business days (Calculated at checkout)

Express Shipping

  • Global Express: 2-4 business days (Calculated at checkout based on weight/destination)

Note: Customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the shipping cost.
