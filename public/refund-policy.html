Refund Policy

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026  |  EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 1, 2026

✅ We stand behind every pair we sell. If you're not completely satisfied, we'll make it right — no hassle, no fine print excuses.

1. Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a full refund under the following conditions:

  • The item is returned within 14 days of the delivery date
  • The item is unworn, unwashed, and in its original condition
  • All original tags are still attached to the garment
  • The item is returned in its original packaging (if available)
  • A valid order number and proof of purchase is provided

2. Non-Refundable Items

  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered
  • Items with removed tags or damaged packaging due to customer negligence
  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or heavily discounted (more than 50% off)
  • Gift cards and store credit vouchers
  • Custom or bespoke tailored orders
  • Items damaged due to misuse or incorrect care

3. Refund Timeline

Refund Method Processing Time Credit Time
Original Payment Method (UPI/Card/Netbanking) 1–3 business days after receipt 3–7 business days
Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders 3–5 business days after receipt 5–10 business days via bank transfer
Store Credit / Gift Voucher Instant upon approval Immediate

⚠️ COD orders — please provide your bank account details (account number & IFSC code) when initiating your refund request so we can process your bank transfer promptly.

4. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, please:

  • Visit our Order Tracking page and use the "Request Return" button
  • Or email us at harry23yt@gmail.com with your order number, reason, and photos if applicable
  • Or call us at 907617347 during business hours (Mon–Sat, 10am–7pm IST)

Once your return request is approved, we will arrange a pickup or provide a prepaid return label at no charge to you.

5. Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with:

  • Your order number
  • Clear photographs of the damage or defect
  • A brief description of the issue

We will arrange a full refund or replacement at our expense with priority shipping, no return required for severely damaged goods.

6. Refund Denial

We reserve the right to deny a refund if the item shows clear signs of use, washing, or alteration beyond inspection, or if the 14-day return window has passed. We will always communicate our decision clearly and in good faith.

7. Shipping Costs

  • Original shipping charges are non-refundable (unless the error was ours)
  • Return shipping for defective/wrong items is fully covered by Harry & Co
  • For change-of-mind returns, the customer bears the return shipping cost
