Return & Exchange Policy

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026  |  EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 1, 2026

♻️ 14-Day Hassle-Free Returns. We make returns and size exchanges simple, because great denim should fit you perfectly — or we'll sort it out.

How It Works — 4 Simple Steps

1
Request
Submit your return/exchange via our order tracking page or by contacting support
2
Pack
Pack the item securely with tags intact in original or similar packaging
3
Ship
We arrange a free pickup from your address (for defects) or you drop it off
4
Resolved
Refund processed or new size dispatched within 3–5 business days of receipt

Return Eligibility

  • Returns must be initiated within 14 days of the delivery date
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition
  • All original tags must be attached
  • Items must be free of odours, stains, or visible signs of wear
  • A valid order number must be provided

Exchange Policy

We offer free size exchanges for all items within 14 days of delivery. If your size is out of stock, we will:

  • Notify you when the requested size is restocked
  • Offer a full refund
  • Offer a store credit with a 10% bonus value as a token of appreciation

Non-Returnable Items

  • Items marked "Final Sale" (clearly indicated on product page)
  • Custom, bespoke, or personalised orders
  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered
  • Items with removed tags
  • Socks, undergarments, and accessories for hygiene reasons

Defective or Wrong Items

If you received a defective, damaged, or incorrect product:

  1. Contact us within 48 hours of receiving the delivery
  2. Email photos of the defect to harry23yt@gmail.com
  3. We will arrange a free return pickup and send the correct item immediately
  4. No return shipping cost to you — ever, for our mistakes

Return Shipping

  • Defective/Wrong Items: Fully covered by Harry & Co — free pickup arranged
  • Change of Mind / Size Exchange: Customer bears the return shipping cost (approx. ₹80–₹150 depending on courier)
  • We recommend using a trackable courier service for your return

Initiating a Return or Exchange

Option 1 — Online (Fastest):

  • Visit Track My Order
  • Enter your order number and email
  • Click "Request Return" or "Request Exchange"

Option 2 — Contact Us Directly:

  • Email: harry23yt@gmail.com
  • Phone: 907617347 (Mon–Sat, 10am–7pm IST)

Returns Support

We respond to all return requests within 24 business hours.
