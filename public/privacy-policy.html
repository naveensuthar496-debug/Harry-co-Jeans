Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026  |  EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 1, 2026

At Harry & Co Jeans ("we", "our", or "us"), your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or make a purchase.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information:

  • Identity Data: Name, username, date of birth
  • Contact Data: Email address, phone number, billing and shipping addresses
  • Transaction Data: Purchase details, order history, payment method type (we do not store full card numbers)
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, device identifiers, time zone
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, products viewed, search queries, session duration
  • Marketing Data: Preferences in receiving marketing communications

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and fulfil your orders, including managing returns and exchanges
  • Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and delivery notifications
  • Respond to customer service enquiries promptly
  • Send promotional communications (only with your explicit consent)
  • Analyse purchasing trends to improve our product selection and user experience
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity, unauthorised access, or illegal conduct
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract: Processing necessary to fulfil your purchase order
  • Legitimate Interests: Fraud prevention, website improvement, and analytics
  • Consent: Marketing emails and newsletters — you may withdraw consent at any time
  • Legal Obligation: Tax records and compliance with Indian law

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Shipping Partners: Courier services require your name, address, and phone for delivery
  • Payment Processors: Secure payment gateways for transaction processing
  • Analytics Services: Anonymised data for performance analysis
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law, court order, or government authority

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies for cart functionality and session management. We may also use analytics cookies to understand how customers use our website. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption, secure database storage, and access controls. However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, typically:

  • Order data: 7 years (for GST and tax compliance)
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Until you unsubscribe
  • Customer accounts: Until deletion is requested

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • Withdraw marketing consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
